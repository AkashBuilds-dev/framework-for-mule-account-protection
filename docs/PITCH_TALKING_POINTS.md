# MuleShield Speaker Talking Points

## Speaker 1 — Problem and solution

**Open with:** “₹48,021 crore lost to bank frauds in FY26. That’s the problem statement SIH26184 gave us.”

- **Use:** ₹48,021 crore; **2.73 million** reported fraud events; **80% Precision@Top-10**.
- “A fraud signal is valuable only if it reaches the person who can interrupt the cash-out.”
- “The **golden hour** is the operating window MuleShield protects.”
- “**Detection to action in two seconds** is the system outcome.”
- Explain the six-step chain: detect → connect → predict → prioritise → freeze → dispatch.
- **Close with:** “MuleShield turns a suspicious transaction into a coordinated intervention.”
- Do not say “we hope” or “we think.” Say “the prototype shows,” “we measured,” or “the system displays.”

## Speaker 2 — Innovation and live demonstration

**Open with:** “Here’s what makes us different.”

- “We identify **five connected mule rings**, not isolated accounts.”
- “We rank **449 possible cash-out cells** and focus response resources on the highest-risk zone.”
- “The prediction path completes in **60 milliseconds** in the prototype.”
- “We find **syndicates, not accounts**.”
- “We **predict, don’t just detect**.”
- Backup technical line: “FastAPI orchestrates model and workflow events; React role dashboards receive them over WebSocket; the field client is a cache-capable PWA.”
- Point to the cascade, not a “cool feature”: alert → auto-freeze request → SLA → dispatch → audit.

## Speaker 3 — Impact, policy, and scale

**Open with:** “Watch the bank laptop.”

- “Within **two seconds**, all **four dashboards** receive the incident.”
- “The Admin Console makes the SLA and the **96%** seeded compliance view auditable.”
- “The bank workflow prepares an action under **Section 102 CrPC**; the final legal decision remains with the authorised officer.”
- “Deployment is **one HQ plus N role clients**—from one control room to a national network.”
- “We complement RBI’s ecosystem; we do not claim to replace it.”
- Do **not** say “like RBI.” Say “MuleShield complements RBI MuleHunter by adding predictive cash-out response.”

## Shared rules

- Lead with **operational intervention**, not AI buzzwords.
- Say “prototype,” “controlled simulation,” and “mock notification” where appropriate.
- Do not claim production integration, live citizen data, or a legally automatic freeze.
- Pause after every major number; let judges read the dashboard.
